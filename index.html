Before you use this map

This is a free reference tool showing the U.S. Geological Survey's estimate of where the August 2016 flood reached along the Amite and Comite rivers, along with today's FEMA flood zones and public parcel records.
  • It is not a flood zone determination, elevation certificate, survey or insurance advice.
  • It does not show flooding from smaller creeks, drainage or ponding, so a property can show dry here and still have flooded.
  • Parcel, owner and FEMA data come live from public sources and may be incomplete or out of date.
  • Always confirm with the seller's disclosure, an elevation certificate, an official flood determination and the property's flood claim history.

August 2016 Flood: Where the Water Was

USGS mapped flood extent along the Amite and Comite rivers, with high-water marks, today's FEMA flood zones, and live parish parcels. Click anywhere on the map for a spot report.

Type an address. You'll get whether the 2016 water reached it, the nearest surveyed high-water mark, and the parcel it sits on.

How to read this. Blue is the USGS estimate of the August 2016 flood extent, built from surveyed high-water marks and ground elevation. It covers flooding from the main river channels inside the dashed USGS study area only. Smaller creeks and local drainage (for example around Walker, Grays Creek, or Jones Creek and Beaver Bayou in Baton Rouge) are not fully modeled. Outside that line, "no blue" means not mapped, not dry. Homes can also have flooded from local drainage or ponding that this map does not show, so treat it as a strong reference and confirm with the seller's disclosure, elevation certificate and flood claim history.

Sources. Flood extent and high-water marks: Heal and Watson, 2017, USGS data release doi.org/10.5066/F79K48C1 (simplified for web display, about 6 m tolerance). FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer (live). Parcels: East Baton Rouge Parish Assessor via EBRGIS, and the public Livingston Parish Assessor Parcels layer on ArcGIS Online (live; check the assessor's site for the latest ownership). Address lookup: Esri World Geocoder.
